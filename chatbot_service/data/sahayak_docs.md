# SAhayak Platform — Knowledge Base

## What is SAhayak?

SAhayak is a web-based accessibility platform designed to help senior citizens, specially-abled individuals, orphan homes, and donors access essential services from a single interface. The platform offers travel assistant booking, caretaker hiring, an open tagged accessibility map, community support, and government scheme information.

---

## User Roles

SAhayak has four types of users:

- **Specially-abled**: Differently-abled individuals who need travel, caretaker, or accessibility services.
- **Senior-citizen**: Elderly users who need assistance with travel, care, and navigating services.
- **Orphan-home**: Organizations representing orphanages who can connect with donors and community support.
- **Donor**: Volunteers and donors who want to support the platform and its beneficiaries.

---

## How to Sign Up

To create a new account on SAhayak:

Step 1: Go to the SAhayak website homepage.
Step 2: Click the "Sign Up" or "Get Started" button.
Step 3: Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
Step 4: Create a strong password and confirm it.
Step 5: Select your Role — choose "specially-abled" or "senior-citizen" if you need services. Choose "donor" if you want to help others. Choose "orphan-home" if you represent an orphanage.
Step 6: Click "Register" or "Create Account".
Step 7: You will be redirected to the login page or your dashboard.

Common sign-up errors:
- "Email already registered" — Use the Login page instead.
- "Passwords do not match" — Re-enter both password fields carefully.
- "Invalid email format" — Check for missing @ or .com in your email.

## How to Log In

To log into your SAhayak account:

Step 1: Go to the SAhayak website.
Step 2: Click "Login" from the navigation bar.
Step 3: Enter your registered email address.
Step 4: Enter your password (passwords are case-sensitive).
Step 5: Click the "Login" button.
Step 6: You will be taken to your personal dashboard based on your role.

If you forgot your password, click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page.

## Home Dashboard

After logging in, you will see your dashboard. The dashboard shows:

- A welcome message with your name.
- Four quick-access cards: Travel and Assistant Booking, Caretaker Services, OTM Accessibility Map, Community Hub.
- A Quick Actions panel for common tasks.
- An accessibility toolbar for text size, language, and voice settings.

## Travel and Assistant Booking Module

### How to Book a Travel Assistant (as a user)

Step 1: From your dashboard, click the "Travel and Assistant Booking" card.
Step 2: Enter your pickup location. You can allow the system to detect your location or type it manually.
Step 3: Enter your destination.
Step 4: Select the date and time of your trip.
Step 5: Optionally add special requirements such as "wheelchair support needed" or "hospital visit".
Step 6: Click "Find Assistants".
Step 7: Browse the list of available assistants. Each card shows name, photo, rating, distance, skills, and cost.
Step 8: Click on an assistant card to view their full profile.
Step 9: Click "Book This Assistant".
Step 10: Review your booking details on the confirmation screen.
Step 11: Confirm the booking.
Step 12: A Booking ID will be generated. Wait for the assistant to accept.

### Booking Status

After booking, your status will change through these stages:
- Pending Acceptance: The assistant has not yet responded.
- Confirmed: The assistant accepted your booking.
- Assistant En Route: The assistant is traveling to your pickup location.
- Trip In Progress: Your trip has started.
- Completed: Your trip is finished.
- Cancelled: The booking was cancelled.

### How to Track Your Assistant

After your booking is confirmed, go to "My Bookings". You will see a live map showing your assistant's location in real time, along with a status panel.

### How to Cancel a Booking

Go to "My Bookings", find the booking you want to cancel, and click "Cancel Booking". Cancellation is only possible before the trip starts.

### How to Rate Your Assistant

After your trip is marked complete, a rating screen will appear. Give a star rating out of 5 and optionally leave a review.

## Caretaker Services Module

### How to Find and Book a Caretaker

Step 1: From your dashboard, click the "Caretaker Services" card.
Step 2: Select the type of care you need (daily care, post-surgery, elderly care, physiotherapy support, medication reminder).
Step 3: Choose duration: Hourly, Daily, or Monthly.
Step 4: Optionally set a gender preference.
Step 5: Enter your location or service area.
Step 6: Click "Search Caretakers".
Step 7: Browse caretaker profiles. Each card shows name, photo, experience, specializations, verification badge, rating, and rates.
Step 8: Click a caretaker card to view their full profile including certifications, past reviews, and rates.
Step 9: Click "Book Now".
Step 10: Fill in start date, duration, address, and any special notes (for example: "patient has diabetes").
Step 11: Click "Send Request". The caretaker will receive your request and respond.
Step 12: Once the caretaker accepts, confirm the booking. A Booking ID will be generated.

### Managing Caretaker Bookings

You can see your caretaker bookings under "My Bookings" with these tabs:
- Pending: Requests not yet accepted.
- Upcoming: Confirmed bookings that have not started.
- Active: Ongoing care sessions.
- Completed: Finished sessions (you can rate from here).
- Cancelled: Cancelled bookings.

You can cancel a booking that has not started from the Upcoming section.
After a session is complete, you can rate the caretaker from the Completed tab.
You can re-book the same caretaker from the Completed tab.

## Open Tagged Map (OTM) Module

### What is OTM?

OTM stands for Open Tagged Map. It is a community-powered map where users discover and tag public places based on their accessibility features — such as ramps, elevators, accessible restrooms, and wheelchair-friendly pathways.

### How to Find an Accessible Place

Step 1: From your dashboard, click the "OTM Accessibility Map" card.
Step 2: The map loads centered on your current location.
Step 3: Use the search bar to type a place name or area (for example: "AIIMS Delhi" or "Connaught Place").
Step 4: Use the filter panel to select accessibility features you need (Ramp Access, Elevator Access, Accessible Restroom, Wheelchair-Friendly Pathway, Low-Height Counters).
Step 5: The map updates to show only places matching your filters.
Step 6: Click on a map pin to open the Place Card showing accessibility score, confirmed features, and reviews.

### How to Tag or Add a Place

Step 1: Go to the Open Tagged Map.
Step 2: Click the "Add a Place" or "Tag a Place" button.
Step 3: Fill in the Place Name, Address, and Place Type.
Step 4: Check all applicable accessibility features.
Step 5: Optionally upload a photo.
Step 6: Click "Submit Tag".
Step 7: Your submission goes into Pending Verification. Community members will review it.

When enough community members confirm it is accurate, it appears on the public map.

The Community Hub allows users to connect with support groups, forums, and community networks. Donors and volunteers can find opportunities to contribute. Orphan homes can connect with the wider support community.

## Accessibility Features

SAhayak is designed to be accessible. The following features are available on every page:

- **Increase Text Size**: Click the A+ button in the accessibility toolbar.
- **Decrease Text Size**: Click the A- button.
- **Language Toggle**: Switch between English and Hindi using the EN/HI button in the top bar.
- **Voice Interaction**: Click the microphone icon on the chatbot to speak your question.
- **Text-to-Speech**: The chatbot can read its responses aloud. Click the speaker icon after a response.
- **High Contrast Mode**: Toggle in the accessibility toolbar for better visibility.

## Common Questions and Answers

**How do I register?**
Click Sign Up on the homepage. Fill in your name, email, phone number, create a password, select your role, and click Register.

**I forgot my password.**
On the Login page, click Forgot Password below the login button and follow the instructions.

**How do I book a travel assistant?**
Log in, go to your dashboard, click Travel and Assistant Booking, enter your pickup location, destination, date and time, then click Find Assistants. Choose one and confirm your booking.

**How do I track my assistant?**
After your booking is confirmed, go to My Bookings. You will see a live map with your assistant's location and a status panel.

**How do I cancel my booking?**
Go to My Bookings, find the booking, and click Cancel Booking. This is only possible before the trip starts.

**How do I hire a caretaker?**
Log in, go to your dashboard, click Caretaker Services, set your care requirements, browse profiles, select a caretaker, and send a booking request.

**What is OTM?**
OTM stands for Open Tagged Map. It shows public places with accessibility features like ramps and elevators. You can also add new places to help the community.

**How do I add a place to the map?**
Go to Open Tagged Map, click Add a Place, fill in the place name and address, check the accessibility features present, and click Submit.

**How do I change the language to Hindi?**
Look for the EN/HI toggle button in the top navigation bar and click it.

**How do I rate my assistant or caretaker?**
After your trip or care session is marked complete, a rating screen will appear. Give a star rating and leave a review.

**How do I become a donor?**
During sign-up, select Donor as your role. You can then access donation features and community support opportunities from your dashboard.
